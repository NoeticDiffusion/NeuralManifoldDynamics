# 137 — 2026-06-26 — 04c window-overlap purity audit

## Session purpose

Science lead requested a window-overlap purity audit before upgrading the
04b Fast/FastDelay > Simultaneous/Slow m/d finding from "promising" to
"internally validated." The concern: 8 s MNPS windows may blend encoding
with adjacent baseline, retention, or retrieval signal.

---

## 1. Encoding overlap geometry (hard constraint)

With 8 s windows and 4 s step, the maximum physically achievable encoding
overlap fraction is bounded by `enc_dur / 8`:

| Condition    | enc_dur | max enc overlap |
|-------------|---------|-----------------|
| Fast         | 2.80 s  | **0.350** (35%) |
| Simultaneous | 2.80 s  | **0.350** (35%) |
| FastDelay    | 6.40 s  | 0.800 (80%)     |
| Slow         | 7.00 s  | 0.875 (88%)     |

Empirical maxima confirm the bound is tight (Fast=0.358, Simultaneous=0.352,
FastDelay=0.806, Slow=0.879). Filters F2 (≥50%) and F3 (≥70%) are therefore
**geometrically impossible** for Fast and Simultaneous — no window can ever
pass them regardless of alignment.

## 2. Contamination profile (mean overlap fraction per condition)

| Condition    | enc   | baseline | retention | retrieval | other |
|-------------|-------|----------|-----------|-----------|-------|
| Fast         | 0.264 | 0.162    | 0.348     | 0.003     | 0.223 |
| FastDelay    | 0.449 | 0.121    | 0.259     | 0.002     | 0.168 |
| Simultaneous | 0.264 | 0.162    | 0.348     | 0.004     | 0.222 |
| Slow         | 0.474 | 0.116    | 0.248     | 0.002     | 0.160 |

For Fast/Simultaneous, each "encoding" window is on average only 26% inside
encoding, with 35% inside retention and 16% inside baseline. This is a direct
consequence of the short encoding duration relative to the 8 s window.

**Verdict: the Fast/FastDelay > Simultaneous/Slow pattern survives all
applicable filters (F0, F1, F4).** Under F1 (window centre inside encoding)
the absolute values increase, indicating the pattern is *stronger* when
windows are better aligned to the encoding interval.

This supports upgrading the 04b result from "promising internal" to
**internally validated** for m and d, with the caveat documented below.

---

## 4. Interpretation and residual caveats

**What the audit confirms:**
- The m/d pattern is not merely a retention or baseline contamination artefact.
- Restricting to windows whose centre falls inside encoding amplifies the effect,
  not attenuates it (F1 medians are uniformly higher than F0 while preserving rank).
- Overlap-weighting (F4) gives consistent results.

**What remains unclear:**
- F2/F3 cannot be applied to Fast/Simultaneous at 8 s windows. A 4 s (or shorter)
  window rerun would allow ≥50% tests for all conditions. The science lead has
  flagged this as the next decision point.
- The FastDelay–Slow comparison under F3 (FastDelay=0.645 > Slow=0.305) is
  consistent with the overall direction but rests on only the two longer-encoding
  conditions; its specificity relative to encoding vs. general "sequential-digit"
  neural processing is not resolved.

**Remaining open claim label:**
The finding is now: **Internal validated result (n=30, Friedman + BH-FDR,
survives F0 / F1 / F4 purity filters; F2/F3 geometrically inapplicable at
current window size).**  Not yet: "pure encoding-phase MNPS elevation."
