# Noetic anchor article handover

## Research question

How should the downstream analysis repo and article-writing path be structured
now that the MNDM 2.3 embodied-anchor surface is implemented and validated on
`ds003838`?

## What was produced

Created:

- `project/ideas/Noetic Anchoring Dynamics/article_handover.md`

## Handover emphasis

The handover argues for an **interaction paper**, not a theory-overclaim paper.

Recommended core framing:

- `anchor_state` is additive and time-aligned beside MNPS
- it is distinct from `feature_anchors`
- the first article should show interaction with:
  - `mnps_3d`
  - `coords_9d`
  - Jacobian/MNJ summaries
- the first positive result should come from sustained-task `block_native`
  analysis, not from the most fragile dynamical surface

## Analysis-repo recommendations

The handover is written for:

- `J:/repos/NoeticDiffusion/`

It recommends:

- a geometry-aware loader stack
- a primary concatenated `block_native` table for `ds003838`
- 3D, 9D, and MNJ analyses in that order
- null controls before strong interpretive claims

## Article recommendation

Strongest paper shape:

1. show embodied anchors are measurable and QC-aware
2. show they vary with task state/load
3. show interaction with `m/d/e`
4. show stronger or more specific effects in 9D
5. show more cautious MNJ/deformation associations

## Key boundary preserved

The handover keeps the same contract boundary as the code/docs:

- embodied anchors modulate and help interpret MNPS
- they do **not** redefine the canonical chart
- they do **not** justify a new canonical fourth axis claim at this stage
